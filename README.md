# LearnoryX

## Autonomous Agentic AI for the Germany Applicant Journey

LearnoryX is an autonomous agentic AI platform designed to help applicants from India navigate their journey toward **higher education, vocational training, internships, and employment opportunities in Germany**.

Instead of providing only chatbot-based answers, LearnoryX analyzes an applicant's profile and documents, identifies missing requirements, evaluates readiness, discovers relevant opportunities, creates a personalized roadmap, and recommends the applicant's next best action.

---

## Problem Statement

Applicants planning to move to Germany for education, vocational training, internships, or employment often face:

- Complex eligibility requirements
- Multiple document requirements
- Qualification and recognition challenges
- Language requirements
- Difficulty finding suitable opportunities
- Information spread across multiple websites
- Uncertainty about what to do next
- Lack of personalized guidance

Most existing solutions provide information but do not continuously analyze the applicant's current state and guide them through the complete journey.

---

## Our Solution

LearnoryX acts as an **AI-powered applicant journey agent**.

It converts applicant information into an intelligent, personalized journey.
